Meet Maria

Maria B Guinle is a certified Pilates instructor with 10 years of practice, based in San Diego, California. She believes wellness should never be complicated: move your body, laugh often, enjoy your life.

Her teaching is built on Joseph Pilates' “Contrology” — moving with control, intention and precision — and on one simple promise: Pilates meets you where you are.
